# Email Account Setup Guide

## Basic setup

Use only the organization's approved email application.

1. Open the approved email application.
2. Select Add Account.
3. Enter the company email address.
4. Enter the authorized password.
5. Complete MFA if required.
6. Allow the account to synchronize.

## Security

Never provide your email password to another person.

Do not configure company email on an unauthorized device unless company policy permits it.

## Troubleshooting

If account setup fails, contact IT support and provide the displayed error message.
