# Password Reset Guide

## Problem: User forgot their password

Use the organization's approved password-reset process.

1. Open the official account recovery page.
2. Enter the company username or email.
3. Complete identity verification.
4. Follow the password-reset instructions.
5. Create a new password according to company password requirements.
6. Sign in again.

## Security rules

Never share passwords with IT staff or other users.

IT support should not ask users to provide their existing password.

Do not use passwords that are easy to guess.

## Account locked

If the account is locked, contact IT support or follow the organization's approved account-unlock procedure.
