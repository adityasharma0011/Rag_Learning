# Printer Troubleshooting Guide

## Problem: Printer is not printing

Check:

1. Printer power.
2. Network or USB connection.
3. Printer paper.
4. Printer error messages.
5. Ink or toner status.
6. Print queue.

## Clear a stuck print job

1. Open printer settings.
2. Open the print queue.
3. Identify the stuck document.
4. Cancel the document.
5. Try printing again.

## Network printer

Verify that the computer and printer are connected to the appropriate network.

## Escalation

Contact IT support if:

- The printer shows a hardware error.
- The printer cannot be detected.
- Multiple users cannot print.
- The problem continues after clearing the print queue.
